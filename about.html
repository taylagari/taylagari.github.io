About Us

Crafting unforgettable journeys since 2010. We believe travel transforms lives—and we’re here to make every trip extraordinary.

Our Story

Founded by two passionate travelers in 2010, Wanderlust Expeditions began as a small local tour operator in Bali. Today, we curate bespoke experiences across 30+ countries—with sustainability and authenticity at our core.

We don’t just book trips—we build connections between cultures, communities, and curious explorers like you.

Why Travel With Us?

We’re guided by values that put you—and the planet—first.

Sustainable Travel

Local Expertise
